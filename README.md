# Privacy Policy for P2 PRIME GO

Last updated: 25/09/2026

Perodua Sales Sdn Bhd operates the PRIME GO mobile application.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Our application authenticates users exclusively via an internal LDAP (Lightweight Directory Access Protocol) network. 
* **Authentication Credentials:** When you log in, your username and password are used solely to authenticate your identity against the secure LDAP server. 
* **Data Transmission:** These credentials are encrypted in transit and are never stored on external third-party servers, rented, or sold.

## 2. Security
We value your trust in providing us your authentication details, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 3. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
